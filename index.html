City Stickers is a project that explores Mainz through the world of street stickers. I believe that if we allow something to appear on the walls of our city - whether it's a statement or a piece of art - it becomes a reflection of who we are, our opinions, and our beliefs, for better or worse.
I found Mainz to be full of opinions. It was fascinating to learn about the city and its people through this practice of looking closely at something many people usually overlook. Stickers might seem small or unimportant, but they often carry interesting and unexpected stories.

This archive is just a starting point. It could grow endlessly, but for now, it reflects my short stay in the city.

The What

>>>Sticker art, also known as slaps in a graffiti context, is a form of street art [1] in which an image or message is publicly displayed using stickers. These stickers may promote a political agenda, comment on a certein policy or issue, or comprise a subcategory of graffiti [2].

>>Sticker artists may hand-draw stickers, print them using a commercial printing service or at home with a computer printer and self-adhesive labels, or have them made commercially.

>Any kind of blank sticker can be used for sticker art. Both name tags and Label 228s [3] are often used with hand-drawn art, and are quite hard to remove, leaving a white, sticky residue. Eggshell stickers are also a popular a type of sticker created specifically for street art. They are named because an attempt to remove them results in tiny pieces breaking off, like an eggshell. Eggshell stickers are made of a mixture of paper and plastic which protects them from the elements. Eggshell stickers longevity allows sticker art to be a part of many urban landscapes [4].
